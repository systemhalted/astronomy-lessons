  predicted 283″/day against the Pisces field stars.

  reused whenever an angular estimate is needed in the field.

  by Session 8's seeing calculation. No measured split verdicts are recorded.

  Session 9 supplies a worked hypothetical image calculation.
- *Stacking and sky background* — Session 9 proposes NGC 7331 sub-stacks and an
  M33 depth test. Measured SNR and sky-background ratios remain unrecorded.

* A note on provenance

Sessions 1 through 5 were written as chat sessions and were not saved at the
time. These files reconstruct them from the session log kept in memory, which
recorded each week's concept, exercise, retrieval questions and sky data. The
physics, the figures and the observing plans are as delivered; the prose is
rebuilt rather than original. The answer sections for Sessions 1 through 4 were
worked out fresh here, since only the questions were logged. Session 6 is the
original text.

Sessions 7–9 were imported from the linked Google Drive documents on 2026-11-05.
Session 7 uses its embedded Org source once, without duplicating the rendered
lesson. Its appendix claims that source already existed in GitHub, but the
checked main revision contained only Sessions 1–6. Sessions 8–9 were converted
from document text. Original prose, calculations, questions and historical sky
predictions are retained; each new lesson has a separate source and accuracy
review section. Review notes distinguish corrections from the historical text.
